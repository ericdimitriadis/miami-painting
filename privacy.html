Privacy Policy

How information is handled on this independent painting and pressure washing guide.

← Back to Home

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit this independent Miami painting and pressure washing information website or call the number displayed on it. The website is independently operated and is not affiliated with the former Superior Painting Services Miami business.

1. Introduction

This website provides information about painting, pressure washing, and related services. It also helps visitors make telephone inquiries that may be referred to independent service providers. This policy applies to information handled in connection with the website and those inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website and whether telephone lead-handling services are enabled, the following types of information may be collected:

  • Information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, location, and project requirements.
  • Telephone numbers, caller identification, and basic call details, such as call time and duration, when available through the telephone service.
  • Information you share during a telephone inquiry, such as the service you need and when you want to be contacted.
  • Technical information that hosting or telecommunications providers may process, such as IP address, browser type, device details, and general website usage or server logs.

The current website does not have a visitor submission form. Providing information through a telephone inquiry is optional, although certain details may be needed to help with your request.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, identify relevant independent providers, and help arrange a requested estimate, service discussion, or consultation. It may also be used to evaluate inquiry quality, maintain or improve website functionality, detect misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This is an informational and lead-generation website, not necessarily a direct provider of the services described. When you call the displayed number, your inquiry may be connected or referred to an independent painting, pressure washing, or related service provider. Information relevant to your inquiry may be shared with that provider so it can respond to your request.

The independent website operator may receive compensation for generating or referring qualifying telephone inquiries. The providers are separate businesses, are responsible for their own services, estimates, and agreements, and may have different privacy practices.

Making an inquiry does not, by itself, authorize unrelated promotional calls, texts, or emails. Any additional marketing contact must have a separate lawful basis, including consent where required.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone inquiries may be routed or tracked through third-party telecommunications or call-handling services. If those services are used, call-related information such as the originating number, time, duration, routing, and referral outcome may be processed.

This policy does not mean that every call is recorded. If call recording is introduced or enabled, appropriate notices and consent procedures must be used where required by law. Callers should receive any required notice before a call is recorded.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The current website files do not include advertising pixels or optional website analytics scripts. Basic technical data may still be handled by a hosting provider to deliver the site, keep it secure, or maintain server logs. If cookies or additional analytics tools are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe their use and any available choices.

7. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with relevant independent service providers to help fulfill a visitor's inquiry, and with website hosting, call-routing, and other technical providers as needed to operate the site and telephone service. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to address fraud, abuse, or security concerns.

Provider access should be limited to information reasonably relevant to the service request or operational need.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled by the website and its service providers. No website, telephone system, or method of storage is completely secure.

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or to meet legal and operational requirements. Exact retention periods may depend on the website operator and any service providers involved.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may be entitled to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. You may also choose not to provide optional information during an inquiry.

Privacy requests should be directed to the independent website operator using the privacy contact details once published below. A request may require verification, and the operator can only address information it controls. Independent service providers may need to be contacted separately regarding information they hold.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Businesses reached through telephone referrals and websites linked from this site may have their own privacy notices and information-handling practices. Please review their policies before sharing sensitive information or entering into a service agreement.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website, telephone lead-handling arrangements, or information practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date when appropriate.
