FAQs

What to Know Before Getting Started

What painting services are commonly available in Miami?

Depending on the provider, services may include residential and commercial interior and exterior painting, cabinet painting, deck painting and associated preparation work.

How long does a typical painting project take?

Timelines depend on the area's size, surface preparation, weather, product drying times and the availability of the contractor. Ask for an estimate specific to the project.

Are color consultations available?

Some painting providers offer color consultations or guidance on paint shades and finishes. Confirm this when discussing your project.

Are lower-VOC or environmentally conscious paint options available?

Many paint products include lower-VOC options. Suitability and availability vary, so confirm the product specifications with a painting professional.

What preparation may be needed before painting?

Preparation can include cleaning, fixing surface defects, sanding, masking adjacent areas and priming. The appropriate work depends on the condition and type of surface.
